## Strategy in one breath

Weekday = **intensity indoors** (Kickr, 60 min, measurable watts). Weekend = **volume outdoors** (Z2 miles + long-ride progression). **MAP is the lever** — until MAP rises, FTP won't (MAP can't sit below 115% of FTP), so weekday work goes MAP/VO2 → threshold → event-specific, *not* endless tempo. Polarize: hard days truly hard, easy days truly easy.

### ⚠️ The #1 behavior change: slow the long rides down

Your 43.7 mi long ride ran avg HR 157 = **Zone 3 tempo** — too hot for base miles. But your long-ride **decoupling is negative** (you get *stronger* late) → the aerobic base is genuinely good and you're leaving free gains on the table. Ride long days in **Z2 (138–151)**. Feels too easy. That's the point: more fat-burning, less fatigue, longer rides, faster recovery. Save the suffering for the Kickr.

### 🩹 Knee-recovery protocol (added 2026-07-07, still active)

Injured ~6/30; two 1-week breaks (wk5 + wk6). Back riding 7/13, pain-free. First comeback ride (7/13, "Ramp It Up Lite") was textbook: cadence 85 rpm, decoupling −17.5% (stronger late). Coming-back rules, biggest levers first:

1. **Spin, don't grind.** High cadence (90+ rpm), low resistance. High-torque mashing is the #1 knee stressor → **ease off MAP/threshold intensity for ~1–2 weeks** (those are grind-heavy). Rebuild Z2 base before resuming hard Kickr intervals.
2. **Bike fit.** Saddle too low = classic front-of-knee (patellar) pain; too high = back-of-knee. Worth a fit check *before* ramping volume.
3. **Full warm-up** before any hard effort — never cold knees into intervals.
4. **Strength mods.** Keep knee-friendly **hip-hinge** work (RDL, hip thrust); **back off deep loaded knee flexion** (squats, Bulgarian split squats) until the knee is quiet. Re-add gradually.
5. **Pain rule:** dull ache = manage (cadence/fit/load); **sharp pain = stop the ride.** Don't push through sharp knee pain.

## Strength training (2×/wk, work gym, 12 PM)

Evidence-checked add-on (cycling is the lowest-interference endurance sport — this is additive to FTP). **Mon = Session A (heavy), Wed = Session B (lighter)** — keeps bike-quality days protected; Friday stays lift-free before the Sat long ride. Starts **wk of 6/15** (after the 6/9 Full Frontal).

**Session A (Mon):** squat 3×8-10→4×4-6 · RDL 3×8-10→3×5 · hip thrust 3×10→3×6-8 · plank 3×30-45s · Pallof 3×8-10/side.
**Session B (Wed):** Bulgarian split squat 3×8/side · KB swing/DB RDL 3×10 · ham curl 3×10 · side plank · back-extension holds. Keep moderate (protects Thu).
**Warm-up (5 min):** half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch · glute bridge · cat-camel · world's greatest stretch · bird dog.

**Progression/periodization:** wks 1–3 acclimation (light, RIR 3-4, technique) → heavy (80-90%) by mid-July → **drop to 1×/wk maintenance (Mon) from ~7/20** (keep load, cut volume). Diablo wk (8/10) light, no loaded legs 3-4 d before the 8/15 climb. **Drop lifting entirely 5-7 d before the 9/5 century** (8/31 Mon skipped). Real heavy block belongs in the off-season (post-9/30).

**Cautions:** judge interference by *power, not soreness*; a bike fit is likely the biggest low-back lever; prioritize protein while cutting to protect lean mass; don't fear small leg-mass gain (usually power-positive).

## Weight loss

Volume + Z2 does most of it. Two rules: **don't over-fuel easy rides** (water + maybe a banana on Z2 ≤ 90 min → taps fat), but **do fuel the Kickr days and anything > 2 hr** (60–90 g carb/hr) — bonking long rides wrecks recovery, and consistency is the whole game. ## Checking in — how to maintain continuity

This plan does **not** depend on keeping one chat alive (long chats get compacted and unwieldy over 4 months). Continuity is three durable layers:

1. **Memory** — `~/.claude/.../memory/training_plan_2026.md`. Every **new** chat auto-recalls the plan + your baselines.
2. **This repo** — `training-plan-2026.md` (this file) + `src/plan_check.py` (the tracker). Version-controlled, chat-independent.
3. **Live warehouse** — auto-ingests from Strava daily (GitHub Actions cron), so actuals are always current.

**Recommended cadence:** start a **fresh chat** each weekly check-in. First message: *"how am I tracking vs the plan?"* → I run:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m src.plan_check          # status today
.venv/bin/python -m src.plan_check --asof 2026-07-15
```

…read memory, and give you ahead/behind + CTL ramp + any adjustment. Questions like *"did my Z2 discipline improve?"*, *"am I behind pace?"*, *"update my zones from the new test"* all work from a cold start.
