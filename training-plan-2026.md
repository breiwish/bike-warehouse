# Road Training Plan — Summer 2026

**Window:** 1 Jun → 30 Sep 2026 (17 weeks)
**Rider:** LTHR 171 · resting HR 55 · FTP-est ~159 W (HR-modeled — Full Frontal sets the real number)
**Gear:** Wahoo Kickr Core 2 + Zwift + SYSTM. Power is **Kickr-only** (no outdoor PM → outdoor rides go by HR).

## Goals (all of them)

1. **1200 mi** ridden by 30 Sep (≈ 10 mi/day, ~69 mi/wk average).
2. **Get faster / more watts** — raise FTP via MAP-first indoor work.
3. **Lose weight** — volume + Zone-2 fat oxidation.
4. **Complete a century** (100 mi) — Labor Day weekend.
5. **Climb 3–4000 ft** — Mt. Diablo (~3,800 ft); Mt. Hamilton if going for 4,000+.

---

## Strategy in one breath

Weekday = **intensity indoors** (Kickr, 60 min, measurable watts). Weekend = **volume outdoors** (Z2 miles + long-ride progression). **MAP is the lever** — until MAP rises, FTP won't (MAP can't sit below 115% of FTP), so weekday work goes MAP/VO2 → threshold → event-specific, *not* endless tempo. Polarize: hard days truly hard, easy days truly easy.

### ⚠️ The #1 behavior change: slow the long rides down

Your 43.7 mi long ride ran avg HR 157 = **Zone 3 tempo** — too hot for base miles. But your long-ride **decoupling is negative** (you get *stronger* late) → the aerobic base is genuinely good and you're leaving free gains on the table. Ride long days in **Z2 (138–151)**. Feels too easy. That's the point: more fat-burning, less fatigue, longer rides, faster recovery. Save the suffering for the Kickr.

## Weekday Kickr workouts (specific, by block)

All names verified against the current SYSTM library (durations are SYSTM nominal). Tue = MAP/VO2, Thu = Threshold/sweet-spot. Within a block the workout stays fixed on purpose — repeating the same session is how you *see* the watts climb.

| Block (weeks) | 🔴 Tue — MAP/VO2 | 🔴 Thu — Threshold | Alt / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** wk 1–4 (base) | **MAP Progression 1 (NoVid)** · 45m — 2×8×30/15, gentlest MAP entry | **The Way Out** · 60m — sweet-spot, low-stress | alt Tue *Revolver* 45m (15×1); alt Thu *G.O.A.T.* 42m |
| **B2** wk 5–8 (MAP build) | **MAP Progression 3 (NoVid)** · 50m — 2×12×30/15 | **Defender** · 59m — 4×10 over-unders | alt Tue *Revolver* |
| **B3** wk 9–12 (FTP build) | **A Very Dark Place** · 54m — 5×4min VO2 | **Defender** · 59m — over-unders | alt Tue *Nine Hammers* 55m; alt Thu *Fight Club* |
| **B4** wk 13–17 (event) | **Nine Hammers** · 55m — mixed threshold+VO2, race-real | **Fight Club** · 60m — threshold + surges (century feel) | alt Tue *A Very Dark Place* |

**Half Monty as a workout (you asked for this):** it's a ramp that *also* refreshes FTP/MAP/LTHR — so use it on a Tuesday in place of the MAP session whenever you want a benchmark. Scheduled drops: **wk 6** (formal mid-test) and **wk 9** (recheck entering the FTP block). Don't burn it in the wk 13–17 peak.

**Climb rehearsal (for Mt. Diablo, wk 11):** in wk 9–10, swap one **Thu** to **Thin Air** · 50m (a single sustained ~35–40 min near-threshold climb) to practice steady climbing power.

**Bad-weather / recovery indoor:** endurance → **A Sunday in Hell** · 60m (steady aerobic). Recovery → **Recharger** · 30m (the only true recovery workout in SYSTM — extend with a free-ride if you want 45m+).

> ⚠️ Verified-pool caveats: SYSTM's *recovery* catalog is one-deep (Recharger, 30m) and its sub-60-min pure-*endurance* catalog is thin — both confirmed by research, not invented. Plenty of MAP/threshold depth, which is what this plan leans on.

## Weight loss

Volume + Z2 does most of it. Two rules: **don't over-fuel easy rides** (water + maybe a banana on Z2 ≤ 90 min → taps fat), but **do fuel the Kickr days and anything > 2 hr** (60–90 g carb/hr) — bonking long rides wrecks recovery, and consistency is the whole game. Warehouse tracks `calories` per ride.

## Checking in — how to maintain continuity

This plan does **not** depend on keeping one chat alive (long chats get compacted and unwieldy over 4 months). Continuity is three durable layers:

1. **Memory** — `~/.claude/.../memory/training_plan_2026.md`. Every **new** chat auto-recalls the plan + your baselines.
2. **This repo** — `training-plan-2026.md` (this file) + `src/plan_check.py` (the tracker). Version-controlled, chat-independent.
3. **Live warehouse** — auto-ingests from Strava daily (GitHub Actions cron), so actuals are always current.

**Recommended cadence:** start a **fresh chat** each weekly check-in. First message: *"how am I tracking vs the plan?"* → I run:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m src.plan_check          # status today
.venv/bin/python -m src.plan_check --asof 2026-07-15
```

…read memory, and give you ahead/behind + CTL ramp + any adjustment. Questions like *"did my Z2 discipline improve?"*, *"am I behind pace?"*, *"update my zones from the new test"* all work from a cold start.
